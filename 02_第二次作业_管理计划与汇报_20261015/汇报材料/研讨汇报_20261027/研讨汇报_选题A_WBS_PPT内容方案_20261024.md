### 页 3 · 课件怎么讲 WBS（Ch5）

- 块 1｜定义（s20）：WBS = 面向可交付成果的分解，定义项目总范围；work package = WBS 最低层任务；范围基线 = 批准的范围说明书 + WBS + WBS 词典
- 块 2｜建法（s26）：guidelines／analogy／top-down／bottom-up 四种；**我们采用 top-down**：从 4 个一级包往下拆
- 块 3｜词典（s29~30）：任务模糊则无法估时估成本；每项必须写入 WBS 词典
- 强调框（s31 原文摘录，英文照抄+中文对照）：
  - "A unit of work should appear at only one place in the WBS."
  - "A WBS item is the responsibility of only one individual, even though many people may be working on it."
  - 译文：一项工作在 WBS 中只能出现一次；每个 WBS 条目只由一人负责——即使多人协作。

### 页 6 · 分歧的实质：卡在课件的哪条建议上

- ①张的写法把「按分类维护」当一项工作内容，但用例层没有这个包 → 触碰 s31："A unit of work should appear at only one place in the WBS."（一项工作只出现一次）
- ②归属之争：**按产出物归属**（知识条目维护归系统建模线）vs **按知识分类归属**（归知识库内容线）
- ③两个解法与代价（依据 s32：WBS 须容纳变更并保持工作内容受控）：
  - 解法一：删「按知识分类」五字，回归「维护知识条目」——成本最低
  - 解法二：走变更控制，新增「知识分类维护」用例与工作包——改动大但语义完整
- 结论行：这不是谁的错，是执行层与课件建议的差距；按变更控制收口

# 附录 B · 课件原文摘录（Ch5，实测 verbatim）

- **s20**：A WBS is a deliverable-oriented grouping of the work involved in a project that defines the total scope of the project. A work package is a task at the lowest level of the WBS. The scope baseline includes the approved project scope statement and its associated WBS and WBS dictionary.
- **s26**：Using guidelines / The analogy approach / The top-down approach: Start with the largest items of the project and break them down / The bottom-up approach: Start with the specific tasks and roll them up / Mind-mapping approach.
- **s29**：Many WBS tasks are vague and must be explained more so people know what to do and can estimate how long it will take and what it will cost to do the work. A WBS dictionary is a document that describes detailed information about each WBS item.
- **s31**：A unit of work should appear at only one place in the WBS. The work content of a WBS item is the sum of the WBS items below it. A WBS item is the responsibility of only one individual, even though many people may be working on it. The WBS must be consistent with the way in which work is actually going to be performed.
- **s32**：Project team members should be involved in developing the WBS to ensure consistency and buy-in. Each WBS item must be documented in a WBS dictionary. The WBS must be a flexible tool to accommodate inevitable changes while properly maintaining control of the work content.
- **s33**：Scope creep…resulted in the bankruptcy of a large pharmaceutical firm, Texas-based FoxMeyer Drug. McDonald's…After spending $170 million on consultants and initial implementation planning, realized that the project was too much to handle and terminated it.
